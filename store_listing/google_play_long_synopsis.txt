Shuffle Master is an interactive card tool for exploring a standard 52-card deck, understanding perfect Faro shuffles, following binary shuffle sequences, predicting card positions, and playing five-hand Black Jack with Faro-ordered decks.

A perfect Faro shuffle is deterministic: it rearranges the two equal halves in a precise interlacing pattern rather than randomly mixing the cards. Shuffle Master makes those permutations visible and lets you follow how cards move through the deck. Card and shuffle tools work locally; Contact and Inquiries are online features that require an internet connection.

VISUALIZE PERFECT FARO SHUFFLES

Step through both major Faro methods:

- Out-Faro, where the original top card stays on top
- In-Faro, where the first card from the lower half moves to the top

Advance one shuffle at a time, run the sequence automatically, follow the shuffle counter, or reset the deck. Automatic runs stop on their own when the cycle completes. Animated movement and cycle messages make each change easier to track, and the in-Faro view narrates how a mixed-looking deck gradually realigns and snaps back to its original order. A standard deck completes an out-Faro cycle in 8 shuffles and an in-Faro cycle in 52.

LEARN BINARY SHUFFLE SEQUENCES

Enter a value from 0 to 51 and see its six binary places translated into a sequence of perfect Faro shuffles. Each processed 0 bit applies an out-shuffle, each 1 bit applies an in-shuffle, and higher unused places are ignored. Emulate the sequence to watch the original top card move through the deck step by step; a value of 0 leaves the deck unchanged.

TAKE THE FARO CHALLENGE

Choose Out, In, or Binary. Set 1 to 8 out-shuffles, 1 to 52 in-shuffles, or choose a binary sequence from 1 to 51. The resulting deck stays face down so you can predict a card's location at one of the 52 numbered positions, then tap any position to reveal or hide the card and check your answer. Changing the mode or shuffle value clears revealed cards.

DEAL AN ORDERED BLACK JACK TABLE

Choose In (1-52), Out (1-8), or Binary (1-51) to build the ordered deck, then tap Deal to give two cards to each of five players and the dealer. Play the highlighted hand with Hit or Stand; a bust or 21 ends that turn automatically. After all five players finish, the dealer hits on 17 or less unless every player busted. Non-busted player hands are marked Win, Lose, or Push. Continue deals another round when at least 12 cards remain; Shuffle rebuilds the selected order and clears the round. Your game progress and settings are saved locally and restored when you return.

CUSTOMIZE AND EXPLORE

Browse the complete deck grouped by suit and choose from 10 card-back designs. Your selected design is remembered and used in the Faro Challenge and Black Jack views.

The app has nine tabs: Card Backs, Card Deck, Out-Faro Shuffle, In-Faro Shuffle, Binary Shuffle, Faro Challenge, Black Jack, Information, and Help. Contact sends the name, return email, question, and displayed app diagnostics you submit to the developer. Inquiries downloads app-filtered submissions and displays app, version, series, content, and date/time, without names or email addresses. Both Information views require internet access. Help explains every tab.
